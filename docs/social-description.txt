MEDIUM POST (LinkedIn / Product Hunt / Reddit)
----------------------------------------------
Introducing Korio — a private, offline-first focus & productivity app for Windows. ⚡

Korio quietly tracks how long you actively use the apps (and websites) you choose, turns it into clear visuals, and gives you gentle nudges to stay on track — without sending a single byte to the cloud.

What's inside:
• 📊 Automatic focus tracking + a Focus Score from your own app categories
• 🧩 Customizable dashboard (drag-and-drop cards, category pie, heatmap, timeline)
• 🌐 Optional website tracking via a companion browser extension (domains only)
• ⏳ Per-app & per-site daily limits (warn or auto-close)
• 🍅 Pomodoro, stopwatch, timer & world clock — with selectable sounds
• ✅ Tasks, sticky notes, goals & streaks, end-of-day digest, quick-capture hotkey
• 🔒 100% offline, local SQLite, no telemetry — export/backup your data anytime
• 🪶 Lightweight & portable (built with Tauri — runs in ~50–90MB RAM)

The name comes from the Japanese word kōritsu (効率), meaning "efficiency."

Made by Ferdous Utsho.
